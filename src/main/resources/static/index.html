Metacius Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · App package: com.metacius

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Metacius mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Metacius is an academic, non-commercial project built for student research on mental well-being. It helps users record mood check-ins and complete standard self-report questionnaires (PHQ-9, GAD-7, PSS-10 and WHO-5).

By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Mood, sleep and questionnaire data relate to your health and are treated as sensitive information.

Information stored on your device

The App stores a login session (an access token and basic profile details such as your name and username) in the device's local storage so you stay signed in. This is removed when you log out.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your precise or approximate location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • We do not use advertising IDs, advertising SDKs or third-party analytics or tracking tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To save and display your mood check-ins, questionnaire scores and insights.
  • To let the authorised research team review participant results, including generating automated (AI-assisted) summaries of questionnaire scores for research analysis.
  • To produce research findings, which are reported only in anonymised, aggregated form.
  • To keep the App working, secure and free of abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or marketing.

4. Sharing of information

Your information is accessible only to the Metacius project team. We share data only in these limited cases:

  • Service providers: our backend is hosted on Render (render.com), which stores and processes data on our behalf. AI-assisted summaries may be produced by an AI service provider acting on our behalf, which receives only the scores needed to create the summary.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, regulation or a valid legal request.
  • Research outputs: only anonymised, aggregated results that cannot identify you.

5. Data security

All communication between the App and our server is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Access to the research server is limited to authorised team members. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and research data for as long as your account is active or as needed for the research project. Journal entries and chat messages are not sent to our server and are cleared when you log out or close the session. When the research project ends, or when you ask us to, we delete your personal data from the server.

7. Your rights and account deletion

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop using the App at any time. You may request to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your account and all associated data.

To delete your account, email sravanthi.puli88@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (or include your registered username) with the subject "Delete my Metacius account". We will delete your account, mood check-ins and questionnaire history from our server within 30 days and confirm by email. Anonymised research results that can no longer be linked to you may be kept.

8. Not a medical or emergency service

Metacius is a research and self-reflection tool. Questionnaire results are screening scores, not a diagnosis, and the App does not replace a qualified doctor, counsellor or therapist. The App is not monitored and cannot respond to emergencies.

If you are in distress or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency services right away. In India you can call Tele-MANAS on 14416.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
